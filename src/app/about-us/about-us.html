Meet Our Founder

Isreal Felix Isaac (Felmanic)

Felmanic Brandz was founded by Isreal Felix Isaac, widely known as Felmanic — a visionary entrepreneur with a strong passion for fashion and retail display innovation. With years of industry experience, he established the company with a clear mission: to deliver high-quality mannequins and retail display solutions that combine durability, modern design, and affordability.

Through hands-on leadership and a commitment to excellence, Felmanic continues to drive the brand forward — building lasting relationships with clients while maintaining uncompromising standards in quality and service.
